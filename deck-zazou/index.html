The Team

Hosted by people
who live it.

Movement — Workout Lead

Hermione Plumptre

A former England lacrosse player and professional 400m hurdler ranked top 30 in the UK, Hermione has 15 years of coaching experience across Barry's, Soho House, adidas and on film sets for Warner Brothers and Sony Pictures. Her programming spans strength, HIIT, running, mobility and recovery — opening up new ways of moving and a deeper understanding of what your body is capable of. As a foodie athlete, the right nutrition has always mattered as much as the training. She also founded She Can Do It All, the first Female Coaches Empowerment Union.

Food — Dinner Lead

Sara Barto

Sara's path to food is as rich as the cooking itself. She spent the early part of her career in film production before following her deeper calling to the kitchen in 2020, and has since supported restaurant concepts across Europe as a hospitality consultant. Her cooking draws from the ingredient-led traditions of the American Southwest and the precision of Korean culinary heritage — protein-centric, technically considered, and worth sitting down for. At PLUTO, Sara is the reason food is never an afterthought.

Brand & Growth

Carmen Jenny

Carmen has spent her career at the intersection of storytelling, community and growth. A second-time founder, she grew a community of 40k+ users in under 18 months before scaling Scripe to one of Germany's fastest-growing AI startups. Named Forbes 30 Under 30, Leader of Tomorrow by the St. Gallen Symposium, and Executive Lead at Sigma Squared Society. She found PLUTO as a guest — one of the original regulars — and never really left. At PLUTO she leads brand and growth, bringing the infrastructure to take the PLUTO Method from Berlin to the world.
