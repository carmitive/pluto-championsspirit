The retreat

Six pillars.
One unforgettable experience.

◎

Movement

Led by Hermione, professional athlete and coach, every session is designed to push and restore in equal measure. She meets everyone where they are and trains people to perform. Technical, intentional, and built around the belief that real performance starts with balance.

◎

Food & Table

Our chef Sara designs every meal to feel like a special occasion. Protein-rich, seasonal, and always generous — eaten together around the table.

◎

Recovery & Rest

High performers rarely give themselves permission to stop. PLUTO builds recovery in as a pillar, not an afterthought — because rest is where adaptation actually happens. From mobility to spa treatments, stillness and off time.

◎

Intention

No agenda slides. No optimisation frameworks. Just good people, good food, honest conversation, and the kind of joy that only shows up when the pressure is off. We move together, we eat together, we laugh together — and we leave with something that no productivity hack has ever delivered.

◎

Community

Every attendee arrives by invitation. What happens between them — over a training session, around the dinner table, on a walk back from the water — is something no itinerary can manufacture. The connections formed tend to outlast the trip, and reunite at the next one.

◎

Place

No matter where a PLUTO retreat takes place, the location is always part of the experience. With Champion Spirit, the retreat will be rooted in the exceptional surroundings of the Bahamas — where tranquility and performance meet, and reset happens when the environment insists on it.

The team behind PLUTO

Built by people who
live it.

Movement & Performance

Hermione Plumptre

A former England lacrosse player and professional 400m hurdler ranked in the top 30 in the UK, Hermione has spent 15 years coaching across Barry's, Soho House, Adidas, and on feature film sets for Warner Brothers and Sony Pictures.

Her programming meets people where they are — covering HIIT, strength, speed, mobility, and recovery — always rooted in the belief that real performance comes from balance, not burnout. As a foodie athlete, nutrition has always been as central as the training. Her mission with PLUTO is to bring people back to a little more balance with both food and fitness.

She also founded She Can Do It All, the first Female Coaches Empowerment Union, creating a space for mentorship and collaboration among women in the industry.

Food & Community

Sara Barto

A well-renowned European chef whose path to food is as rich as the cooking itself. Sara spent the early part of her career in film production before following her deeper calling to the kitchen full-time in 2020. As a hospitality consultant, she has supported restaurant concepts across Europe, helping them find their culinary identity.

Her cooking is shaped by the bold ingredient-led traditions of the American Southwest, the precision of Korean culinary heritage from her mother, and years of hosting across cultures. The result is food that is protein-centric and technically considered, but never clinical — generous, seasonal, and worth sitting down for.

At PLUTO, Sara is the reason food is never an afterthought — proof that nourishing and indulgent are not opposites, and that the table is just as important as the training.

Brand & Growth

Carmen Jenny

Carmen has spent her career at the intersection of storytelling, community, and growth — beginning as a journalist and influencer before building two startups. As a second-time founder, she grew a community of 40,000+ users in under 18 months, before scaling Scripe to one of Germany's fastest-growing AI startups.

Named Forbes 30 Under 30, Leader of Tomorrow by the St. Gallen Symposium, and Executive Lead at Sigma Squared Society.

She found PLUTO as a guest — one of the original regulars from the very first events — and never really left. At PLUTO she leads brand and growth, bringing the infrastructure and narrative to take the PLUTO Method from Berlin to the world.

The partnership

What we envision
together.

We've designed this partnership to be as straightforward as possible. Here's what we give, and what we'd need in return.

What PLUTO brings

Our contribution

  • → Full retreat concept, programming, and logistics managed end-to-end by PLUTO
  • → A curated group of 10–20 high-net-worth founders and executives
  • → Professional athlete-led fitness programming across 7–10 days
  • → Sara-curated culinary experiences for every group meal

  • → Active introduction of guests to Champion Spirit membership

What we ask for

Venue requirements

  • → Room block: 10–20 rooms for 7–10 nights at a negotiated group rate
  • → Exclusive or priority access to gym and fitness facilities during morning sessions
  • → Group spa access — either dedicated time slots or a curated spa programme for attendees
  • → Access to outdoor spaces — beach, pool, or grounds — for group activities and meals
  • → F&B collaboration: flexibility for our chef to work alongside your kitchen team for group dinners
  • → A venue liaison point of contact throughout the planning and retreat period
