How I Work

The same process, every time

Every project starts the same way – with a conversation, not a content calendar.

01

Deep-Dive Interview

An unscripted, recorded conversation to understand your story – where you come from, what you've actually done, and why it matters. This becomes the raw material for everything that follows.

02

Positioning & Strategy

A full strategy for your personal brand and your offer – who you're actually speaking to, what they want to hear, and the messaging and narrative that makes it land.

03

Content Strategy

The positioning becomes a system – content pillars, formats, and a rhythm that's realistic to keep up.

04

Then, It Branches

From here we decide where the story actually gets told – LinkedIn, Instagram, your website, or a combination. The channel changes; the process that got you there doesn't.
