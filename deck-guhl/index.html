The Idea

Haircare where it belongs –
right after the workout.

When Guhl shared the Hair Care Club concept, it resonated immediately. PLUTO was built on the exact same belief: that recovery, care, and coming together with great people should follow movement that feels good. We've been running different event formats such as Snack Runs and Supper Clubs across Berlin – bringing together driven people for shared workouts and great food. The "Move & Refresh" format fits this perfectly – creating a genuine, unforced moment for haircare that participants will actually remember and talk about.

The Format

One evening.
Four chapters.

17:00

Arrival & Aperitivo

Guests arrive to a warm welcome, drinks, and light bites. Guhl products are present as part of the setting, making them part of the experience starting from the first moment.

17:30

Move – with Hermione

A 60–75 minute guided session with professional athlete and coach Hermione Plumptre. Movement, mobility, stretch – designed to complement the refresh that follows.

19:00

Hair Refresh – with Guhl

Post-workout, guests freshen up with Guhl haircare kits. The most genuine moment possible to discover what the brand is about – because it's needed, not handed over.

The Guhl Moment
19:30

Dinner – by Sara

A shared dinner crafted by chef Sara Barto. Family-style, seasonal, generous. The kind of food that brings people together and keeps them at the table well into the evening.

The Team

Hosted by people
who live it.

Movement – Workout Lead

Hermione Plumptre

A former England lacrosse player and professional 400m hurdler ranked top 30 in the UK, Hermione has 15 years of coaching experience across Barry's, Soho House, Adidas and on film sets for Warner Brothers and Sony Pictures. Her programming spans strength, HIIT, running and mobility. She'll shape the movement session to complement the Hair Refresh that follows – building anticipation into the workout itself.

Food – Dinner Lead

Sara Barto

Sara's path to food is as rich as the cooking itself. She spent the early part of her career in film production before following her deeper calling to the kitchen, and has since supported restaurant concepts across Europe as a hospitality consultant. Her cooking draws from the American Southwest and Korean culinary heritage – protein-centric, technically considered, and worth sitting down for.

Brand & Growth

Carmen Jenny

Carmen grew a community of 40k+ users in under 18 months before scaling Scripe to one of Germany's fastest-growing AI startups. Named Forbes 30 Under 30 and Executive Lead at Sigma Squared Society. She leads brand and growth at PLUTO. She understands exactly what Guhl is building, and believes PLUTO is the right partner to make it real.

The Venue

Two options.
Both exceptional.

We've identified two Berlin venues that fit the format, the energy, and the community. We're happy to move forward with either – or discuss which feels most aligned with the Guhl brand.

Option A

Freunde von Freunden

Loft Space  ·  Prenzlauer Berg, Berlin

A beautifully curated creative loft with genuine character – light, airy, and intimate. Used by Berlin's design and culture community for editorial shoots and private gatherings. The space feels like a private home rather than a hired venue, which is exactly the tone we want to set for the Hair Refresh moment and dinner.

Option B

Club, Kitchen & Loft  ·  Mitte, Berlin

What PLUTO Brings

More than an event.
A proof of concept.

Full Event Execution

As event partners, PLUTO takes care of the concept, programming, guest curation, and on-the-ground coordination. Guhl brings their own photographer and videographer to capture the evening – and we'll make sure every moment is worth capturing.

An Authentic Product Moment

The Hair Refresh happens post-workout – the most genuine moment for haircare. Participants reach for the product because they actually want it. That's the difference between a brand experience and an advertisement.

Community & Tastemaker Reach

15–20 curated people with real influence over their networks. Influencers, creatives, founders – the kind of voices whose genuine recommendation actually changes what people buy. Organic content follows naturally from the evening.

A Pilot with Series Potential

As discussed, we see this event in October as a pilot. If it goes well the format is ready to repeat. Different Berlin venues, different editions, the same quality of curation.
